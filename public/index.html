How it works

1. Import a video.
2. AI Auto-process — auto-captions in the spoken language, plus clip + B-roll suggestions.
3. Trim / cut — drag the edges of the blue Video block, or Split then delete pieces.
4. Tweak captions and B-roll (drag on the preview, edit in the inspector).
5. Export to a .webm with everything burned in. Keep the tab focused.
